about

Hello! My name is Chris. I'm an illustrator and graphic designer based in Fredericksburg, Virginia — born and raised here, actually. FXBG native through and through.

My background as a visual artist is in illustration and oil painting. Graphic design caught my eye back in 2016 and I've been pursuing a professional career as a designer ever since. I'm still in the process of finding my niche, which is really just a fancy way of saying I'm down for whatever you've got to throw at me.

the work

I work across fine art, illustration, branding, print, packaging, and social. Some of the projects I've gotten to make recently include rebrands for American Trench and I.R.E., packaging for Glade Creek Cider and Domik Foods, a print piece for Sey Coffee, a tri-fold brochure for BTRET, and a postage-stamp series celebrating Fredericksburg. The gallery has more.

let's talk

I'm currently open for commissions and collaborations. The contact page has my email and phone — I'd love to hear from you.
